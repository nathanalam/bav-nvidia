Quick Pricing Formulas

Simple formulas calibrated to the residual income model — use with quarterly earnings reports for rapid fair value estimation

These formulas were derived by regressing the full residual income model's fair value outputs against publicly reported financial metrics. They approximate the full model to within $0.20-2.00 per share. Recalibrate annually as growth rates and margins evolve.

How to Use After Earnings

Quick Decision Framework

  • Step 1: Read quarterly revenue (NVIDIA/AMD) or operating income (Intel) from the earnings press release — available within minutes of the report.
  • Step 2: Annualize (multiply by 4) and divide by shares outstanding.
  • Step 3: Plug into the formula to get fair value.
  • Step 4: Compare to current market price:
    • Market price >15% above fair value → Potentially overvalued
    • Market price within ±15% of fair value → Fairly valued
    • Market price >15% below fair value → Potentially undervalued
  • Step 5 (optional): For higher precision, wait for the 10-Q filing (a few weeks later) and use the 2-variable Ohlson formula (EPS + BVPS) for <$0.30 error.

Back-Calculation: What Revenue Does the Market Imply?

Invert the formula to see what quarterly revenue the current market price implies, then compare to analyst estimates or your own expectations.

NVIDIA: Implied Q_Rev = (Market Price - 37.86) ÷ 1.77 × 24,514 ÷ 4
Example: If NVDA trades at $65 → Q_Rev = (65 - 37.86) / 1.77 × 24,514 / 4 = $93,923M/quarter implied

AMD: Implied Q_Rev = (Market Price - 19.47) ÷ 1.25 × 1,636 ÷ 4
Example: If AMD trades at $55 → Q_Rev = (55 - 19.47) / 1.25 × 1,636 / 4 = $11,654M/quarter implied

Intel: Implied Q_OI = (Market Price - 8.26) ÷ 0.41 × 4,530 ÷ 4
Example: If INTC trades at $10 → Q_OI = (10 - 8.26) / 0.41 × 4,530 / 4 = $4,809M/quarter OI implied

Formula Accuracy vs Model

Limitations & Recalibration: (1) Formulas are calibrated to a specific growth/margin trajectory. If NVIDIA's margins jump to 70% or collapse to 40%, the revenue-based formula will over/under-estimate. (2) Share counts change — update the divisor when there are material buybacks or issuance. (3) The intercept term ($37.86 for NVIDIA) embeds the terminal value assumption. If long-term growth expectations shift, recalibrate. (4) Recalibrate annually by re-running the full residual income model with updated base-year data. (5) For Intel, if the turnaround meaningfully exceeds or disappoints vs the assumed margin recovery, the formula range will shift.
